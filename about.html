Rev. Solomon & Angela Ani

General Overseer

Rev. Solomon Ani and Rev. Angela Ani began their years of service to God in Jos, in the northern part of Nigeria. Great Covenant Dimension Ministry Int'l was born out of a vision God gave Rev. Angela Ani, our Mummy in the Lord. When the call became clear, they put a stop to all their businesses and gave themselves fully to God and to the ministry.

Blessed with five beautiful children, they have raised their family and the church side by side. Today, over ten years later, thousands of souls have been impacted and many lives blessed. They give God the glory for His love and faithfulness as the ministry continues to grow from grace to grace.
